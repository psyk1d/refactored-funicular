Reel Framer

Your hook on top, the clip in the middle, your logo below. Everything is made in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

1Add your clips

Clips that already have a caption on a white background (and maybe a logo) are cut down to the picture, so your hook and your logo replace the old ones.

2Hook and post caption

Inside Claude, the hooks are written by looking at one frame of each clip. Elsewhere they come from built-in templates for your niche. You can edit every line.

Hooks, one per line

Tap an emoji to add it at your cursor. If you have more clips than lines, the last line is reused.

Post caption and hashtags

This is the text you paste under the post, with hashtags. It is not drawn on the video. Each finished video gets a Copy caption button.

4Logo

Raise the strength if a faint box or halo is left. Lower it if parts of the logo start to disappear. The page behind it is always white.

5Quality and export

4K gives crisp text and logo and a higher bitrate. It cannot add detail that the original clip does not have, so a 720p clip stays a little soft; Sharpen adds some crispness. 4K needs a fast computer. If your device cannot keep up, Sharpen switches itself off during the render; if motion still looks choppy, pick 2K or Full HD.

6Links and posting

YouTube makes links in the description clickable. Instagram does not make links in captions clickable, so the Instagram caption has no links; put yours in your bio.
